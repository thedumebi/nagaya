What we help with

Four parts of the same journey. Most people need help with all of them, but you can come to us for one.

Choosing where to apply

A shortlist of schools and courses that fit your grades, your budget and what you want to do afterwards. Including the ones you would not have found on your own.

The application itself

Personal statement, references, transcripts, English tests and the deadlines that go with them. We read your drafts and tell you honestly what is not working yet.

After the offer

Picking the right offer, deposits, your CAS or I-20, and getting financial evidence into the shape the embassy actually expects to see.

The student visa

The application, the documents that go with it, and what happens at the appointment. Nobody should walk into that interview guessing.

Questions

The ones we get asked first.

Which countries do you cover?

Mostly the UK, Ireland, Canada and the United States. If you have somewhere else in mind, ask. If we do not know that system well we will tell you, rather than learn it on your application.

When should I start?

Earlier than you think. Most intakes want applications nine to twelve months ahead, and funding deadlines usually fall before admission deadlines. If you are already late, email anyway. Plenty is still possible.

What does it cost?

It depends on how much of it you want us to carry. Tell us what you need in your first email and you get a price before any work starts. The first conversation costs nothing.

Can you guarantee me an offer or a visa?

No, and you should not trust anyone who says they can. Those decisions belong to the university and the embassy. What we can do is make sure your application is the strongest honest version of itself, and that nothing gets refused for a reason that was avoidable.

What should I put in the first email?

Your most recent results, what you want to study, the countries you are considering, the intake you are aiming for, and anything you are already worried about. Attach your transcripts if you have them to hand.
